# Scrapline — Map Design

## Status

Working design. Not yet frozen.

## High-Level Layout

The current direction is a compact scrapyard arena built around a strong central combat landmark, with four loosely distinct surrounding combat zones and a broken perimeter route for flanking.

The layout should feel irregular and assembled from believable scrapyard structures rather than like a clean four-quadrant arena.

## Working Combat Districts

Potential environmental identities:

- vehicle / scrap yard
- loading / container yard
- collapsed workshop
- makeshift industrial or shanty structures

These are working roles, not mandatory visual themes. They should blend naturally rather than read as four disconnected biomes.

## Center

The center should contain one memorable landmark or wreckage composition.

Possible direction:
- collapsed industrial structure
- crane / loading feature
- scrap-processing area
- major wreck

The center should attract combat without becoming permanent dominant high ground.

## Route Structure

Target route types:

- direct routes into center
- cross-map sightlines broken by meaningful hard cover
- lateral routes between combat districts
- a broken outer-ring flank route
- limited interior / under-structure shortcuts where supported by the chosen assets

Avoid long empty travel lanes.

## Verticality

Use roughly three practical height bands:

1. Ground level — primary combat space.
2. Mid level — roofs, catwalks, container tops, partial second floors.
3. Limited high positions — a small number of exposed perches with multiple counter-angles.

No single tower or roof should dominate the whole arena.

## Spawning

Working direction:
- distributed spawn points around outer and mid-map areas
- roughly 12–20 candidate spawn locations, subject to final player count
- fast access to cover
- avoid obvious spawn rooms
- minimize direct spawn-to-enemy sightlines using available UEFN spawn logic and placement

Desired rhythm:

**spawn → reach cover immediately → choose center or flank → fight**

## Scale

The map should be small enough to sustain frequent combat but large enough to prevent constant unavoidable spawn pressure.

Exact dimensions are intentionally not locked yet. They should be derived after the approved asset kit is inspected in-editor so the layout fits real asset scale rather than arbitrary measurements.

## Art / Composition Rule

Build the finished visual space directly from approved assets.

Do not use blank boxes, primitive-block architecture, or newly authored Blender environment pieces as substitutes for missing art.

The one-shot workflow succeeds only if asset research is completed before implementation.
